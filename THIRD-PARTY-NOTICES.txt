Anatria3D — licensing notice
============================

Anatria3D is educational software. It is NOT a medical device, and it is not
for diagnosis or treatment of any person.


The 3D meshes and the anatomical nomenclature shipped with this application are
not original to it. They come from two unrelated open datasets, under two
different licences, and they are kept in separate files for that reason.

     CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/

Female atlas — Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
------------------------------------------------

Files: `anatomy/manifest_female.json`, `anatomy/*_female.glb`

The trunk — spine, abdominal organs, pelvis and breast, 264 structures. Not a
whole body, because no open dataset provides a female one.

  1. Visible Human Female
     U.S. National Library of Medicine, National Institutes of Health
     The Visible Human Project — the dataset everything below derives from.
     https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/visible/visible_human.html

  2. Human Reference Atlas 3D Reference Organ Library
     HuBMAP Consortium / NIH — https://humanatlas.io/
     Digital object ref-organ/united-female v1.5
     CC BY 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

  3. Anatria3D adaptations — 264 of 888 structures selected, node hierarchy
     flattened with transforms baked in, materials and UV sets dropped, lossy
     Draco compression, stable organ identifiers added, and each structure
     matched by hand to a Terminologia Anatomica term.

     As adaptations of CC BY 4.0 material these files remain CC BY 4.0.

Redistributing these files requires preserving this attribution. There is no
share-alike obligation on them.

**The two atlases must not be merged into one file.** Folding CC BY material
into a CC BY-SA work forces share-alike onto it, imposing a condition the HRA's
authors chose not to impose.

The full notice ships alongside the data, in `anatomy/NOTICE` and
`anatomy/LICENSE`.

The nomenclature of both is Terminologia Anatomica 2 Latin and clinical English
only. No localised medical terminology is stored or redistributed here;
translation is performed at runtime by the AI assistant for the reader in front
of it.

Bundled components
------------------

The application bundles a Python runtime and third-party Python packages, each
under its own licence. Their licence files are installed alongside them, under
`anatria-engine/_internal/`.


Your own data
-------------

Notes, saved sessions and case results are written to a local SQLite database
in your user profile. Nothing is uploaded. The only data that leaves the machine
is the text of a question you send, which goes to the AI provider whose API key
you supplied.
